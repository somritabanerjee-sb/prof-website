LISTENING AS RESEARCH

Music, Memory & Cultural Worlds

Dr. Dripta Piplai (Mondal)'s engagement with music emerges from her broader interest in language, oral history and cultural memory. Her work explores how songs preserve histories, identities and forms of knowledge that often remain outside conventional archives.

Her research particularly engages with Rabindrasangeet, oral traditions and the musical practices of communities whose cultural histories are transmitted through performance and memory.

01

RABINDRASANGEET

Song as Cultural Memory

Rabindrasangeet offers a rich site for thinking about language, performance, memory and cultural identity. Through song, literary language becomes embodied, performed and continually reinterpreted.

This intersection between language and music forms part of a wider interest in the relationship between linguistic practices and cultural memory.

RESEARCH & DOCUMENTATION

Songs as Living Archives

Musical traditions can function as living archives. Songs transmit language, histories, emotions and social memory, while performance allows these traditions to change across generations and communities.
