Research Team

Scholars working across language, multilingualism, education, language documentation and cultural research.

The research group brings together doctoral scholars and student researchers working across diverse areas of linguistics and the humanities.

Their work engages with questions of multilingualism, language policy and planning, linguistic documentation, education, community-based research, oral history and cultural memory.
